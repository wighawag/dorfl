Found in the CI sandbox `wighawag/dorfl-ci-sandbox` (private, dorfl `main@e972795d` installed from a tarball, workflows generated by `install-ci`), while exercising the split CI workflows end to end.

In the sandbox lock, agent and apply job logs, most `>>` lines appear twice in a row (for example `>> handed over intake-task for issue:1` twice, `>> locked issue:1 at ...` twice). Probably the phase driver both notes a message and prints its result message. Cosmetic, but it doubles log noise.
