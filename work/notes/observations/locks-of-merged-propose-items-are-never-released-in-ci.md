---
title: 'In CI, the per-item lock of a propose item stays on the arbiter after its PR merges'
slug: locks-of-merged-propose-items-are-never-released-in-ci
date: 2026-09-29
status: spotted
---

Found in the CI sandbox `wighawag/dorfl-ci-sandbox` (private, dorfl `main@e972795d` installed from a tarball, workflows generated by `install-ci`), while exercising the split CI workflows end to end.

After propose PRs #9 (build) and #10 (tasking) merged, `refs/dorfl/lock/task-add-lower-rest-option-to-capitalize` and `refs/dorfl/lock/spec-text-layout-module-wrap-pad-and-align` stayed on the arbiter through later `advance-lifecycle` ticks. The design says such a lock is released "by the next claim (or `dorfl status --reconcile-locks`)", but a merged item is terminal and never claimed again, and no generated job runs the reconcile. They block nothing (terminal items are not enumerated) but accumulate forever. Suggest the `reap-merged-branches` job (or the lock phase) release locks whose item is terminal on `main`.

Update, same day: the two locks were later gone, but no CI job log shows releasing them (not the item runs, not `reap-merged-branches`). The likeliest releaser is a verb run locally against the sandbox in between (`dorfl promote`, or the merge-question surfacer run by hand), which reconciles terminal locks as a side effect. So in a CI-only repository they would still linger until some claim-like verb runs; the finding stands, with a softer "not released by CI" rather than "never released".
